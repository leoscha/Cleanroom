# Roadmap

The roadmap communicates direction, not a delivery guarantee. Security and
verification quality take priority over dates.

## v0.2 — Production text sanitization

- Reliable TXT workflow, verification, quarantine, policies, and safe reports.
- Local-first Ollama connection management.
- First public open-source release.

## v0.3 — Native PDF support

- Harden and graduate the current text-based PDF implementation.
- Expand mapping, structural verification, fixtures, and compatibility coverage.
- Continue rejecting scanned/image-only documents until OCR is available.

## v0.4 — Review UI

- Local review experience for quarantined findings and policy decisions.
- Privacy-safe job navigation and operator approval flows.

## v0.5 — OCR

- Explicit, local OCR pipeline for images and scanned PDFs.
- Confidence-aware mapping and verification before output release.

## v1.0 — AI Privacy Gateway

- Stable gateway contracts, deployment guidance, and production hardening.
- Broader document workflows with auditable policy enforcement.
